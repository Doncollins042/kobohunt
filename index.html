          • CSR-funded scholarship dropsA company funds a term of drops as its social programme. You run it for a management fee and send back an impact report.

          Settle these before launch

          • Keep hunting free.Selling a head start or a wider radar turns the hunt into a paid prize game, which lottery regulators treat differently. Sell looks, never odds.
          • Do not hold the money yourself.Run wallets and payouts through a licensed payment provider, and verify identity before a first withdrawal.
          • Plan for fake GPS.Location spoofing is the main fraud here. Reject mock locations, check walking speed between claims, and tie one account to one device and one bank account.
          • Many small drops beat one big one.A single large drop sends a crowd to one spot. Spread value across places and times, keep drops off roads, and run them in daylight.
          • Sell the sponsor strip directly.Mainstream ad networks restrict rewarding people for keeping ads on screen. Read their policy before plugging one in.
          • Pick your own name.KoboHunt is a placeholder. The notebook title borrows another company's trademark, and the sponsors shown are invented.

          Confirm the legal points with a Nigerian lawyer.
